Passion project · Game

Gravity Sandbox

A gravity playground I built. Place stars and planets, fling asteroids, and watch orbits, slingshots and collisions play out under real N-body gravity. Then land on any world in 3D and explore it on foot.

  • Real conditions. Each world's gravity, temperature, air and oceans come from the simulation, so a jump on a small asteroid can carry you into space.
  • Vehicles. A rover, a hoverbike and a lander you can fly to orbit, then on to other worlds in system flight.
  • Domed bases. Every world has a glass-domed station with an airlock, residents to talk to, missions, upgrades and day–night routines.
  • Built from scratch. Plain JavaScript and Three.js, with every sound synthesized live in the browser.

Passion project · Horror game

Lights Out Delivery

A first-person night-shift horror game that runs in the browser. You drive the night shift for Hollow Creek Parcel Co. while the van drives itself down an empty motorway. You have the mirrors, the headlights, the door locks and a CB radio that sometimes tells the truth. Survive until 6 AM.

  • Five nights, five threats. The Passenger, the Hitchhiker, Static Man, the Other Van and the Lodger. Most are easier to hear than to see.
  • A rhythm-game radio. Hit notes in time to decode warnings about what's coming.
  • Gas station stops at 3 AM. Walk around in first person and buy from a tech tree of 10 upgrades at the till.
  • Secrets. Five hidden lore tapes, two endings and a Custom Night with sliders for every threat.

Passion project · Simulation

Petri Selection

A 3D natural-selection simulator. Little blobs hop out of the rim of a petri dish each morning to forage. Eat one piece of food to survive, eat two to reproduce. Offspring inherit speed, size and sense with small mutations, and the energy cost of each trait decides which ones win over the generations.

  • Real trade-offs. Every step costs size³ × speed² + sense energy, so fast, big blobs run out quickly.
  • Predators and prey. A blob 20% bigger can eat a smaller one, and smaller blobs flee from bigger ones they sense.
  • Live charts. Population, average traits and a speed-vs-size scatter update every day.
  • Run your own experiments. Change the food supply, day length, mutation strength and which traits can evolve.

Passion project · Simulation

Trust Tribes

An evolution simulator about trust. Sixty creatures share a dish, and whenever two meet they play several rounds of the prisoner's dilemma: each secretly chooses to cooperate or cheat. The best earners have children and the worst die out, so you can watch which personality takes over the colony.

  • Six tribes. Mirror, Forgiver, Grudge, Saint, Cheat and Coin, each with its own colour, shape and rule for when to trust.
  • Real game theory. Both cooperating earns +2 each, but cheating someone who trusts you earns +3 and leaves them on −1.
  • Surprising results. Cheats win when nobody meets twice, Mirror wins long games, and Forgiver beats Mirror when mistakes happen.
  • Six experiments. Change rounds, mistakes and rewards, or find the knife edge where the winner changes from run to run.
