Trust Tribes

Sixty creatures share a dish. Whenever two of them bump into each other, each one secretly chooses to cooperate or cheat. The creatures that earn the most points have children, and the ones that earn the least die out. Which personality takes over?

The deal every meeting

Each round, both creatures choose at the same time. Working together is good for both, but cheating someone who trusts you is the best single move. That tension is called the prisoner's dilemma, and it shows up everywhere from animal behaviour to countries signing treaties.

If you only meet someone once, cheating always earns more, whatever they do. But creatures who meet again and again can build trust, and that changes everything. Run the dish and see which strategy wins out over many generations.
